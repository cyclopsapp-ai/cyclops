# Cyclops for Windows

**Know what to say next.** Cyclops is a Windows desktop app that helps you during job interviews and work calls. It listens to the call, writes down both sides in real time, and suggests answers built from your own resume and the job description, in about a second.

**[Download Cyclops-Setup.exe](https://github.com/cyclopsapp-ai/cyclops/releases/latest/download/Cyclops-Setup.exe)** · Website: https://getcyclops.pages.dev

---

## What it does

- **Live transcript of both sides.** Cyclops hears your microphone and the call audio, so it works with Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Webex, Slack huddles, or a phone call on speaker.
- **Answers from your own experience.** Add your resume (PDF, Word or text) and the job description; suggestions use your real projects and numbers, not generic advice.
- **Auto Answer.** Spots questions as they're asked and suggests an answer without you pressing anything.
- **Reads your screen.** One click turns a coding problem, quiz question or slide into a solution with an explanation.
- **Ask anything mid-call.** A chat that already knows the conversation, your resume, and any file you attach.
- **After the call.** Meeting notes, a follow-up email draft, and an honest grade with better phrasing for next time.
- **A small window that stays on top** during the call, plus a dashboard to prepare, rejoin and review sessions.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- A free Groq API key (see below), no credit card needed
- An internet connection during calls
- Headphones are recommended for the cleanest transcript

## Get started

1. **Download** [Cyclops-Setup.exe](https://github.com/cyclopsapp-ai/cyclops/releases/latest/download/Cyclops-Setup.exe) and run it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. **Get your free Groq key** (about 2 minutes):
   1. Go to [console.groq.com/keys](https://console.groq.com/keys) and sign up or log in.
   2. Click **Create API Key** and name it, for example, "Cyclops".
   3. Copy the key right away (it starts with `gsk_`; Groq shows it only once).
   4. In Cyclops, open **Settings → Groq API key**, paste it and click **Save key**.
   5. A green **Connected** badge means you're ready.

   Step-by-step guide with pictures: https://getcyclops.pages.dev/#groq-key
3. **Add your resume** under **Documents**.
4. **Start a session** with **New session** before your call, then click **Start listening** when it begins.
